Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Digital Mom (“Digital Mom”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use this website, send us an enquiry or message us on WhatsApp. We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Digital Mom is the responsible party for the personal information described here.

  • Business: Digital Mom, [registration number to confirm]
  • Address: Centurion, Gauteng, South Africa
  • Information Officer: Charlene, charlene@digital-mom.co.za
  • WhatsApp: send us a message

What we collect

We only collect what we need to reply to you and to do the work you ask for.

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, business name, website or social link, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • WhatsApp messages: your name, phone number and anything you choose to send us.
  • Client work: contact details, billing details and the brand material, logins or content you share with us so we can deliver a project.
  • Website use: if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used, such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser. See our cookie policy.
  • Security data: to stop spam, the contact form uses a short-lived cookie and keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your connection address for a short time.

We do not knowingly collect special personal information (such as health or religious information) or information about children. Please do not send it to us.

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and talk through the work you need (your request and consent);
  • to quote, deliver, invoice and support work you have agreed to (to carry out our agreement with you);
  • to keep the website secure and working (our legitimate interest);
  • to understand which pages are useful and improve the site, only if you accept analytics cookies (your consent);
  • to meet tax, accounting and other legal duties (legal obligation).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you have asked for them. You can stop any marketing at any time by telling us.

Who we share it with

Only the people and services that need it to help us work with you:

  • our website host and email provider, so enquiries reach us;
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us there;
  • Google, for Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies;
  • our accounting and invoicing software, if you become a client;
  • authorities, if the law requires it.

Each of these providers is required to keep your information secure and to use it only to provide its service.

Information sent outside South Africa

Some of these providers, including Google and Meta, store information on servers outside South Africa. When that happens we rely on providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that give a level of protection similar to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client records and invoices: for as long as we work together and then for 5 years, as tax law requires.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google for 14 months, then deleted.
  • Spam-protection data: a few hours at most.

How we protect it

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, keep access to client information to the team members who need it, and use reputable providers with their own security safeguards. If we become aware of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it where we no longer need it or have no lawful reason to keep it;
  • object to us using it, including for marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, including for analytics cookies through .

Contact our Information Officer at charlene@digital-mom.co.za or message us on WhatsApp. We will reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
