Veriserv

A refreshed logo, a new website, a social media campaign and ongoing search care for a business that helps South African companies fund, insure and keep track of their commercial vehicles.

Website redesign

Service
Web Design

The new site gives each part of Veriserv's business its own clear page: finance and insurance, fleet solutions, the K220 and K245C dashcams and a contact page, all built to work well on a phone.

Logo refinement

Service
Graphic Design
What changed
Proportions, slogan position and slogan wording

The icon, name and slogan were rebalanced on a 3x3 grid so the logo reads as one unit, from a website header down to a profile picture. The slogan changed from Sustainable Mobility Solutions to Commercial Mobility Solutions, which says straight away who Veriserv serves.

Digital business cards and email signatures

Service
Graphic Design
Made for
Each director, in the new brand

Digital business cards with a QR code that saves the contact straight to a phone, and matching email signatures with a photo, contact details, social links and Veriserv’s FSP number, so every email carries the new brand.

Social introduction campaign

Service
Social Media Marketing
Platforms
Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok
Format
16 portrait cards, 1080 x 1350

A campaign that introduces Veriserv card by card: the business and its credentials, the finance and risk solutions, then the fleet technology and dashcams, ending with an invitation to get in touch.
