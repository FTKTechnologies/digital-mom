Sacred Sage

A new logo and a 17-card social media campaign for Sacred Sage Rituals, a South African skincare range built around a simple, balancing daily Ritual.

Industry
Natural skincare
Website
sacredsagerituals.com ↗
Social pages

Logo redesign Sacred Sage Rituals campaign

Logo redesign

Service
Graphic Design
What changed
A new icon, cleaner lettering and versions for every size

The round stamp with its tree made way for a new logo built around a crescent moon, a line of balance points and soft waves. The line Beyond Suppression. Into Regulation. stays, now set clearly under the name, with a wide version for headers and a stacked one for profile pictures and packaging.

Sacred Sage Rituals campaign

Service
Social Media Marketing
Platforms
Instagram, Facebook
Format
17 portrait cards, 1080 x 1350

A campaign that explains the Ritual card by card: the brand’s point of view, the simple three-step routine, what each product is for, and where to start, ending with the Ritual Kit.
