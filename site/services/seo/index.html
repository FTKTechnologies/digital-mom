SEO

When someone searches for what you do, your business should be easy to find and easy to understand. SEO is the work behind that: what your pages say, how they are built and how search engines read them. We start with what your customers actually search for, then fix the pages, structure and details that stand in the way.

Depending on the brief, this can include:

  • Keyword and search-intent research around what your customers look for.
  • On-page work: page titles, headings, descriptions and copy.
  • Technical checks on structure, speed, mobile use and indexing.
  • Local search support, including your Google Business Profile where relevant.
  • Content planning for search, and reporting on the data available.

A good fit for

Businesses that want to be found by people already searching for what they do.

Exact deliverables, timing and price are confirmed before any work starts.

How SEO works

Find what people search for.

We research the words and questions your customers type into Google when they need what you do.

Match your pages to it.

Page titles, headings and copy are written so each page clearly answers a search.

Fix what holds you back.

We check speed, mobile use and how search engines read your site, then fix the problems we find.

Check and adjust.

We look at the data available, see what is working and change the plan. SEO takes time, and we never promise rankings.
