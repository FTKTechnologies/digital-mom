Fair questions. Straight answers.

What does Digital Mom do?

Digital Mom is a South African social media and creative team. We help businesses plan and make the content, websites and design behind their digital presence. Depending on the brief, that can include social media marketing, web design, SEO and graphic design.

Do you plan the content, make it and post it?

We can do all three. Before work starts we confirm which channels, deliverables and responsibilities are covered, so you know who is doing what.

Do you build websites and do SEO too?

Yes. Web design and SEO are scoped the same way as everything else: we agree the work, deliverables and price first. We don’t promise rankings, because nobody can honestly guarantee them.

What if we have no idea what to post?

That is fine. We’ll ask about your business, the questions customers ask, what is coming up and what is worth showing. There is usually more to work with than you think.

What do you need from us?

The context only you can provide: what is changing in the business, what matters to customers, which details are accurate and what needs approval. The exact assets, access and feedback depend on the agreed scope.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the type of work, the amount of content or design required, production needs and timing. Once we understand the brief, we confirm the scope and price before work begins. No guessing what is included.

Can you help with a launch or promotion?

Yes. We can scope the creative around the offer, message, audience and channels. The work may include copy, graphics, social content and a landing page, as agreed.

Do we need a long-term agreement?

The right arrangement depends on what you need. Some work is best handled as a defined project; other work needs ongoing support. We’ll discuss the scope that fits the job and confirm it before anything starts.

Can you guarantee more followers, sales or viral content?

No, and nobody honest can. We can agree on the work, deliverables and process, then use the performance information available to guide future decisions.

Do we need to know exactly what we want before contacting you?

No. Tell us what your business is trying to do and what you are struggling to get done. We can help turn that into a workable brief.

What happens after we enquire?

We review the information, ask follow-up questions where needed and talk through the goal and scope. If the work is a fit, we confirm the deliverables, timing and cost so everyone knows what happens next.
