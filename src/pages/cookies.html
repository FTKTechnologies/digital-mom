Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This page lists the ones our website uses. We do not use advertising cookies, and analytics cookies are only set if you say yes.

Your choice

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics cookies. Saying no does not change how the website works. You can change your mind at any time:

Cookies that are always on

These keep the site working and secure, so they do not need your consent.

  • dm_contact: protects the contact form from spam with a single-use token. Deleted when you close your browser.
  • dm-cookie-consent: remembers your cookie choice so we do not ask on every page. Stored in your browser for 12 months.

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and how people find the site, so we can improve it. Google sets these cookies:

  • _ga: tells visits apart. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_*: keeps track of the current visit. Expires after 2 years.

Google processes this information on our behalf, including on servers outside South Africa. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com. If you withdraw your consent, we delete these cookies from your browser.

Other websites

When you click through to WhatsApp or another site, that site’s own cookie rules apply.
