Start with a rough idea.

Here is what happens from your first message to the finished work. You don’t need a polished brief.

  1. 1

    Start with a conversation.

    Tell us what is going on in your business and what you need help with. A rough idea is fine. So is a folder called FINAL_FINAL_USE_THIS_ONE.

  2. 2

    Work out what will help.

    We talk through your goal, audience, current content and the creative work required. If the brief needs sharpening, we do that before jumping into production.

  3. 3

    Agree the scope.

    You receive a clear outline of the agreed work, deliverables, timing and cost. Once the scope is agreed, we can plan the work properly.

  4. 4

    Make it happen.

    We work through the agreed plan, from writing and design to shoots, edits, website pages or ongoing social content. The person who plans the work stays involved while it’s made, so nothing gets lost between the plan and the finished piece.

  5. 5

    Review and keep moving.

    Every post and design waits for you on a shared Trello board. You can comment, ask for a change or approve it there, so feedback stays in one place instead of long email threads. You add the business context only you can provide. We refine the work within the agreed scope and use what worked to plan what comes next.
