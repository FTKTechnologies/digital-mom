Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website. It covers the enquiry form and the contact button. The website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the information you type into the form:

  • your name and email address;
  • your business name, website or social link and the service you need help with, if you fill those in;
  • your message.

Why we use it

We use these details to reply to your enquiry and to talk through the work you need. We do not sell them or use them for anything unrelated to your enquiry.

Who receives it

Your enquiry is emailed to the Digital Mom team. We do not share it with other businesses, except where a service provider that hosts our website or email needs to handle it so that the message can be delivered.

Technical details

To protect the form from spam, the website sets a short-lived cookie that holds a single-use form token, and it temporarily stores a scrambled (hashed) version of your connection address to limit how many messages can be sent in a short time. Neither is used to identify you or track you across other websites.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp at {{phone}} or send an enquiry through the contact page, and we will respond.

Changes

If we change how the website handles your details, we will update this page.
